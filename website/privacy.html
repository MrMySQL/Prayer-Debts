Privacy Policy

Prayer Debts · Effective date: February 21, 2026

Prayer Debts does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Your data stays on your device

All data entered into the app (such as prayer records and progress information) is stored locally on your device.

iCloud synchronization

If iCloud synchronization is enabled on your device, your data may be securely synced across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service. This synchronization is handled entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

No tracking

Prayer Debts does not use analytics services, advertising frameworks, tracking technologies, or third-party data processors.

We cannot access your data

We do not have access to your data at any time.

Deleting your data

If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed from your device. Data stored in iCloud can be managed or deleted by the user through their Apple ID settings.
